# Review the Current preversion

## Agreed direction

Current remains the visual reference. Folio and Nocturne are discarded. Ledger and Atlas are considered only for the search experience.

## Walkthrough

1. Open `index.html`. Assess the overall composition, typography, green card and amount of text.
2. Use **Explore opportunities**. Search for Java; combine role, work mode and salary filters. Remove a filter chip and try an empty result.
3. Switch between **List view** and **Card view**. Both contain the same offers. Decide which should be the default; list is the current default.
4. Open an offer. Review the salary, requirements, company link, fit notes and unknown information. Open the application preview and close it with Escape.
5. Save an offer, visit **Saved**, then remove every offer to see the empty state.
6. Browse **Companies**, search for Valencia and open Vela Systems. Follow one of its open roles.
7. Open **Profile**, change a preference and save it. Check that the summary changes only after saving. Choose a hybrid preference to reveal office days.
8. Use **Explore opportunities** from the profile summary. Its role and work mode should be applied to the search.
9. Repeat the important pages in a narrow window. Use Tab, Enter, Space and Escape to assess keyboard interaction.
10. Use **Reset preview** to restore the initial examples.
